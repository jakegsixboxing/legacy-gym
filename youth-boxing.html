Why we're asking

A space for young people to box

It's a tough time to be young. There's so much crime, violence and confusion around, and a lot of families are doing it tough with less income coming in.

We help young people aged 8 to 18, from all circumstances: a lack of income and support at home, drug and alcohol abuse, and more.

For more than 14 years, Legacy Gym has given young people on the Central Coast a place to box for free. We've always provided that space, and we want to keep providing it. It's our way of doing our bit.

Like every small business, there's only so much we can give away for free, and we've reached it. To extend the program again, we're bringing new sponsors on board. All funds raised through sponsorship go to supporting young people on the Central Coast and keeping them in a boxing gym.

Why you won't see the young people here

We keep their faces off public pages and social media. Their financial situation is sensitive for them and their families, and plenty of the young people involved have come from an extremely tough background.

What you can see are photos from our Fight Nights, where we've raised money to support them.
