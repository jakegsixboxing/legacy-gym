The Philosophy

Most retreats push you harder. We think the point is to come back better.

RESET was built around a simple idea — that real fitness and real rest aren't opposites. Each week blends serious training with genuine recovery and the best of what Queenstown offers, so you leave with more energy than you arrived with. No 5am punishment sessions for the sake of it. Just good, hard work, done properly, in one of the most beautiful places on earth.

The Program

Four days. Four pillars.

Every retreat is built around the same core rhythm — train, move, recover, nourish.

01

Box & Strength

Technical boxing and strength sessions run out of Queenstown's best-equipped performance facilities, coached by local trainers — hard sessions, properly programmed, not random punishment.

02

Mountain & Adventure

Guided hikes, via ferrata and alpine adventure across the Remarkables and the Wakatipu Basin. Big views, real effort, no experience required.

03

Recover

Sauna, cold plunge, private hot pools and bodywork built into every day — recovery isn't an afterthought here, it's on the schedule.

04

Nourish

Meals and evenings built around Queenstown's food and wine scene — this is a retreat you actually enjoy, not one you white-knuckle through.

A Day Here

What a typical day looks like

Structured enough to make progress, loose enough to actually enjoy the trip.

7:00am

Movement & coffee

An easy mobility session followed by good coffee — Queenstown does this better than most places.

7:30pm

Dinner in town

Why Queenstown

The best backdrop for this kind of week

Queenstown does double duty better than anywhere we've found — genuinely serious training and adventure by day, genuinely excellent food, wine and recovery by night.

Train

Where we train

  • The Mill — a purpose-built performance and recovery facility at Remarkables Park, with strength floors, saunas, plunge pools and recovery therapies on site.
  • Industrial Fitness — Queenstown's dedicated boxing and strength studio, coached sessions for all levels.
  • The Remarkables & Wye Creek — rock and alpine terrain for guided climbing and mountaineering.
Recover

Where we recover

  • Onsen Hot Pools & Day Spa — private cedar-lined hot tubs above the Shotover River, with sauna and spa treatments.
  • Ice baths, contrast therapy and massage worked into the daily rhythm, not left as an optional extra.
  • Genuine rest days built into every itinerary — not just a training taper.
Nourish

Where we eat & unwind

  • Rata and Botswana Butchery for standout dinners; Bespoke Kitchen for the mornings after a big session.
  • Bardeaux and The Sundeck for wine, whisky and sunset views over the lake.
  • Queenstown's compact enough that none of this feels like a mission to get to.

These are local favourites we build itineraries around — not official retreat partners unless specifically named as such.
