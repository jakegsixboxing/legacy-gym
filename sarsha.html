The Coach

Pro boxer. Coach.

Sarsha walked into Legacy at 15 years old and has been fighting out of the gym under head coach Jake ever since. Eight years later she holds four current titles and a world ranking — and she’s still climbing.

She’s just returned from a training camp in the USA with Jake and her teammates — her seventh-plus overseas fighting and training camp at just 23, fighting and sparring with some of the best in her divisions across South-East Asia and the United States.

As a coach, Sarsha trains people for fitness and skill and has brought novice amateurs through to the ring — including 1× Australian amateur champion coached under her.
