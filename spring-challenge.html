Session 01 · Team Tonnage Mission

⚙️ Operation Iron Payload

40-Minute Team Mission · Bravo · Charlie · Delta · 10 per squad
🎯 Mission Objective

Shift the greatest combined weight across three lifts:

🏋️ Squat💪 Bench Press🙆 Overhead Press

Each team gets one rack per exercise, plus bars, plates and three tracking sheets / whiteboards.

Phase 1 · 10 Min
🧠 Tactical Preparation
  • Allocate members across the three racks
  • Choose and load your starting weights
  • Decide lifting & rotation orders
  • Plan when and how weights will change
  • Set up a tracking sheet at each rack
  • Lock in your overall plan of attack
🎖️ Leaders & 2ICs organise the strategy — but they lift too.
Phase 2 · 30 Min
🔩 Iron Payload

All three racks run at the same time. After every set, the resting or spotting athlete logs the set just finished:

Weight on bar × valid reps = weight shifted
Squat80kg × 10 reps = 800kg
Bench60kg × 12 reps = 720kg
Overhead40kg × 10 reps = 400kg
Squat + Bench + Overhead = Final Team Score
📋 Rules Of Engagement
  • Every member must lift — Leaders & 2ICs included
  • No dedicated scorer — the resting / spotting athlete logs the previous set
  • The weight of the bar counts
  • Change weights, rotations or strategy whenever you want
  • The 30-min clock never stops — not for plate changes, not for replanning
  • Only controlled, full-range reps count
  • Failed or incomplete reps don't count
  • Spotters, collars & rack safeties must be used
  • Total-volume challenge — not a one-rep-max test
🏆 The Tactical Call

Heavier weights with slower rotations — or moderate weights moved consistently for the full mission? Highest combined weight shifted wins Operation Iron Payload.

Session 02 · Team Endurance Mission

🔥 Operation Redline

35-Minute Team Mission · 5 Min Prep + 30 Min Work · Bravo · Charlie · Delta
🎯 Mission Objective

Bank the highest combined calorie total across two assault bikes — while every athlete keeps cycling through the full endurance circuit.

Each team receives

🚴 2× Assault Bikes🎒 Assigned Sandbags📏 20m Lunge Lane🏃 Run To REVL & Back
🎒 Sandbag Weights
Women10kg or 15kg
Men20kg or 25kg

Assign every athlete's sandbag weight before the mission begins.

Phase 1 · 5 Min
🧠 Tactical Preparation
  • Pick your two starting bike riders
  • Set your initial bike rotation order
  • Assign sandbag weights
  • Organise running groups of up to three
  • Plan how to keep both bikes continuously occupied
  • Agree how the rotation adjusts as fatigue builds
🎖️ Leaders & 2ICs take part in the entire mission.
Phase 2 · 30 Min
🔁 The Circuit
  1. Ride a max of 15 calories on an assault bike
  2. Grab your sandbag and run to REVL and back
  3. On return, complete 20m of walking lunges
  4. You're now eligible to rejoin the bike rotation
  5. Repeat until the 30-minute clock expires
🏃 Running groups: solo or up to three. Groups stay together the whole run, every sandbag completes the full route, and no sandbag gets left on the course.
📋 Rules Of Engagement
  • Both bikes can run at the same time
  • Every member takes part — Leaders & 2ICs included
  • Each bike effort is capped at 15 calories
  • Stop early if you want — but you still owe the sandbag run + lunges
  • No returning to a bike until the full circuit is done
  • Change rotation or strategy whenever you want
  • The 30-min clock never stops
  • Calories mid-rep when the horn sounds still count
  • Unsafe or incomplete work doesn't count
🔢 Final Score
Bike 1 calories + Bike 2 calories = team score
Highest combined calories wins Operation Redline
🏆 The Tactical Call

Keep a constant supply of eligible riders so neither bike is ever left empty. Dead bikes mean dead calories.

Session 03 · Team AMRAP Mission

💥 Operation Ballistic

20-Minute Team Mission · 3 Min Plan + 17 Min AMRAP · Bravo · Charlie · Delta
🎯 Mission Objective

Three minutes to plan. Seventeen minutes to attack. Work together and stack up as many completed rounds as possible before time expires.

Each team receives three dead balls

⚫ 12kg⚫ 15kg⚫ 20kg
Phase 1 · 3 Min
🧠 The Attack Plan
  • Who starts on each ball?
  • How will you rotate?
  • Wait for the lighter ball, or grab the heavier one and keep moving?
  • How will everyone track their completed rounds?
🎖️ Leaders & 2ICs take on the challenge alongside everyone else.
Phase 2 · 17 Min AMRAP
🔁 One Complete Round
  1. 5 throws over the right shoulder
  2. 5 throws over the left shoulder
  3. 5 powerful ball slams
  4. 100-metre run
🔓 The moment you finish your ball work and head out on the run, that ball is free for a teammate. Keep rotating, keep communicating, keep stacking rounds.
📋 Rules Of Engagement
  • Everyone completes and tracks their own rounds
  • Use any available dead ball
  • Change weights between rounds
  • Leaders & 2ICs complete the challenge too
  • The clock never stops
  • Only complete rounds count
  • Horn sounds mid-run? That round doesn't count
🔢 Victory
Every member's completed rounds = team total
Highest total wins Operation Ballistic
🏆 The Tactical Call

Chase the lighter balls for fast, steady rounds — or muscle the heavy 20kg and grind. Whoever keeps every ball moving and never stops rotating comes out on top.
